About Soft Paws

A sanctuary born from compassion.

[PLACEHOLDER] Soft Paws Cat Sanctuary is a volunteer-run rescue organization in Loreto, Baja California Sur, dedicated to providing humane care for cats in need. Founded by passionate animal lovers, Soft Paws operates a cattery where rescued cats receive food, medical attention, and socialization while awaiting adoption.

[PLACEHOLDER] The sanctuary runs on a shoestring budget, relying on donations and the tireless work of its volunteers. Every contribution — no matter the size — directly improves the lives of the cats in their care.

Commercial Laundry Equipment

Loreto, Baja California Sur

Multi-Year Capital Campaign

Property Acquisition — Securing a Permanent Home

Loreto, Baja California Sur  ·  Target: September 1, 2028

[PLACEHOLDER] Soft Paws Cat Sanctuary currently operates on rented property, leaving its long-term future uncertain. This multi-year capital campaign aims to raise $60,000 to purchase the sanctuary's existing property outright, securing a permanent home for the cats in care and the dedicated volunteers who serve them.

Owning the property will eliminate the risk of displacement, allow for future facility improvements, and lay the foundation for a truly sustainable rescue organization in Loreto. The campaign is structured as $20,000 per year over three years, with a fundraising target date of September 1, 2028.

Total Goal: $60,000 USD  ·  $20,000/year  ·  3 Years
