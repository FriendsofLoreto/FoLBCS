Who We Are

A bridge between
two communities.

Friends of Loreto, BCS was founded by people who love the small coastal town of Loreto and wanted to find a meaningful way to support the organizations doing remarkable work there. We are a Minnesota nonprofit corporation, organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

Our model is simple: U.S.-based donors contribute tax-deductible gifts, and we direct those funds to vetted projects that make a real, measurable difference in Loreto — from the cats at Soft Paws to the volleyball courts at UABCS.
