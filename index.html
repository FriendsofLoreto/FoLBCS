Who We Are

A bridge between
two communities.

[PLACEHOLDER] Friends of Loreto, BCS was founded by people who love the small coastal town of Loreto and wanted to find a meaningful way to support the organizations doing remarkable work there. We are a Minnesota nonprofit corporation, organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

Our model is simple: U.S.-based donors contribute tax-deductible gifts, and we direct those funds to vetted projects that make a real, measurable difference in Loreto — from the cats at Soft Paws to the volleyball courts at UABCS.


Support Our Work

Animal Welfare

Supporting Soft Paws Cat Sanctuary with equipment, operations, and a long-term path to owning their property.

Youth Sports

Community Connection

We believe cross-border relationships build stronger communities. Every dollar we raise stays in Loreto.
