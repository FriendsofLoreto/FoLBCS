Tax Deductibility

Friends of Loreto, BCS is recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization. Contributions are tax deductible to the fullest extent permitted by law. You will receive written acknowledgment of your gift for your tax records.

Friends of Loreto, BCS is a Minnesota nonprofit corporation. Please consult your tax advisor for guidance specific to your situation.

Donor Advised Fund (DAF)

Have a Donor Advised Fund with Fidelity Charitable, Schwab Charitable, Vanguard Charitable, or a community foundation? You can recommend a grant directly to Friends of Loreto, BCS.

Use this information when submitting your DAF grant recommendation:

Legal Name: Friends of Loreto, BCS
EIN: 42-2210084
Mailing Address: 1114 Cripple Creek Pass,
                      Lino Lakes, MN 55038

You may specify a grant purpose such as "for Soft Paws Cat Sanctuary programs" or "for UABCS Volleyball." Contact us at admin@friendsofloreto.org if your DAF sponsor needs additional documentation.

Planned Giving & Estate Donations

One of the most meaningful ways to support Friends of Loreto, BCS is through a planned gift — a bequest in your will, a beneficiary designation on a retirement account or life insurance policy, or another estate arrangement. Planned gifts of any size make a lasting difference for the cats of Loreto and the student athletes of UABCS.

Suggested bequest language for your attorney:

"I give to Friends of Loreto, BCS, a Minnesota nonprofit corporation (EIN: 42-2210084), located at 1114 Cripple Creek Pass, Lino Lakes, MN 55038, the sum of $_____ [or _____% of my residual estate] for its general charitable purposes."

To notify us of a planned gift or to request more information, please contact us at admin@friendsofloreto.org. We welcome the opportunity to recognize your generosity and ensure your wishes are honored.
