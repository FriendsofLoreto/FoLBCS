About the Program

Competing with pride, growing with purpose.

The UABCS Loreto campus is home to two competitive volleyball teams — men's and women's — that represent their university and community at the regional collegiate level. For many players, these teams provide their primary access to structured athletics, mentorship, and a sense of belonging.

Friends of Loreto, BCS supports these teams by covering costs that often stand between a student and the ability to compete: league registration fees, team uniforms, and essential equipment. Your donation helps these athletes stay on the court.
